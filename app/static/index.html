It joins as a guest named Uno Notetaker, records and leaves when the call ends. The host may have to let it in.

Add a calendar by its link

Paste your calendar's secret iCal address. Notetaker reads it every few minutes and joins calls that have a link. It's read-only, and you can reset the address in your calendar any time.
